## Base Ingredients
Weetbix mix
- 2 weetbix
- 1/3 cup of milk
Topping layer
- 150g greek yoghurt
- scoop of protein powder

## Alternitives

Tiramisu 
- Replace milk with coffee
- use chocolate protein powder
- dust cocoa powder on top

Double Chocolate
- mix 1-2 tbsp cocoa powder & liquid sweetener with the milk
- use chocolate protein powder
- top with chocolate
